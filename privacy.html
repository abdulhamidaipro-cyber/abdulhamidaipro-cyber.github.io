Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy describes how the "Hamido" application (the "App") handles information when it is used to send and receive WhatsApp messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) provided by Meta.

1. Who operates the App

The App is operated by a single independent operator based in Malaysia who provides business automation services to small businesses. The App is not a public product and has no public sign-up.

2. What information is processed

  • The WhatsApp phone number and profile name of people who message the business number.
  • The content of the messages sent to and received from that number (text, images, voice notes and similar media).
  • Message delivery metadata, such as timestamps and delivery or read status.

The App does not use Facebook Login and does not collect any other personal data.

3. Why it is processed

Information is used only to read incoming messages and to send replies, notifications and service messages on behalf of the business that owns the WhatsApp number. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

4. Sharing

Data is not sold or rented. It is shared only with the service providers needed to run the messaging service: Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) and the hosting providers of the automation software that processes the messages.

5. Retention

Message content is kept only as long as needed to serve the conversation and the business purpose it belongs to, and is then deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights and data deletion

You may ask at any time to see, correct or delete the information held about you. To make a request, send a message to the business WhatsApp number you were in contact with and say that you want your data deleted. The request will be handled and your data removed from our systems within 30 days.

7. Security

Access credentials are stored in restricted configuration and are not shared. Only the operator has access to the systems that process the messages.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
